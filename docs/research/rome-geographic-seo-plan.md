# Rome — geographic and SEO research plan

**Project:** Europe Explorer | **Status:** research baseline | **Date:** 2026-11-28  
**Locales:** cs, de, en, es, fr, it, nl

## Executive decision

Build Rome as one city hub plus geographically coherent area guides. Do not create a separate page for every monument by default.

1. **Ancient Rome:** Colosseum, Roman Forum, Palatine, Imperial Fora, Trajan's Forum, Capitoline Hill and Piazza Venezia.
2. **Historic Centre:** Pantheon, Piazza Navona, Campo de' Fiori, Largo Argentina and nearby squares.
3. **Trevi & Tridente:** Trevi Fountain, Quirinal, Spanish Steps, Via del Corso, Piazza del Popolo, Ara Pacis, Mausoleum of Augustus, Villa Borghese and Galleria Borghese.
4. **Vatican & Borgo:** St Peter's Square and Basilica, Vatican Museums, Sistine Chapel, Borgo, Castel Sant'Angelo and Prati.
5. **Trastevere & Tiber Island:** Trastevere, Santa Maria in Trastevere, Tiber Island, Jewish Ghetto, Great Synagogue and Portico of Octavia. Treat Testaccio as an adjacent candidate, not automatically the same page.
6. **Aventine & Caracalla:** Circus Maximus, Aventine, Orange Garden, Baths of Caracalla and Pyramid of Cestius.
7. **Esquilino & Lateran:** Termini, Santa Maria Maggiore, Esquilino and San Giovanni in Laterano.
8. **Outer archaeological excursions:** Via Appia Antica and Ostia Antica; separate guides only if project POI coverage supports them.

These are editorial visitor zones, not administrative boundaries. Rome is the parent hub; each area page links to neighbouring areas. Individual monument pages are added only when keyword demand and unique editorial value justify them.

Connections to preserve:
- Colosseum, Roman Forum and Palatine are one connected archaeological visitor complex.
- Capitoline Hill and Piazza Venezia continue naturally from the ancient archaeological zone, although the official portal also lists them separately.
- Pantheon, Piazza Navona and Campo de' Fiori are explicitly grouped by the official portal.
- Turismo Roma's 72-hour itinerary says Vatican Museums are about a 10-minute walk from St Peter's Basilica.
- Castel Sant'Angelo connects Vatican/Borgo and the historic centre; cross-link it instead of isolating it.
- Trastevere and Tiber Island are explicitly grouped. The Jewish Ghetto is the natural east-bank counterpart across the river.
- Trevi can be linked to both the historic centre and Tridente.
- Aventine, Circus Maximus and Baths of Caracalla are explicitly grouped.
- Termini / Esquilino / Santa Maria Maggiore / San Giovanni form a distinct eastern visitor corridor.

Do not publish exact walking-minute claims until routes are checked in the app's routing engine. Use official area groupings and map adjacency, not invented travel times.

| St Peter's Basilica | 222,000 |

Source: https://www.ministeroturismo.gov.it/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/TDH_INFOGRAFICA_Aprile_def.pdf

The report says measurement used Italian, English and Spanish keywords and Google Keyword Planner / Destination Insights with Google. It is useful for prioritization, but is not a current volume export by language or country.

Priority from available data:
- **Tier 1:** Colosseum, Pantheon, Trevi Fountain.
- **Tier 2:** Piazza Navona, Galleria Borghese, Vatican Museums.
- **Tier 3:** St Peter's Basilica.
- City and area hubs should also target broad trip-planning intent, not only monument names.

## 3. Implementation order

1. Rome city hub (language-prefixed URL ending in /places/italy/rome).
2. Ancient Rome.
3. Vatican.
4. Historic Centre.
5. Trevi & Tridente.
6. Trastevere & Tiber Island.
7. Aventine & Caracalla.
8. Esquilino & Lateran, subject to map-data coverage.
9. Outer archaeological excursions only if underlying map has sufficient POIs.

Do not create area pages until deep-link focal coordinates and zoom are checked against the live map. The city hub needs a broad but useful historic-centre focal point; each area page needs its own local visitor centre.
